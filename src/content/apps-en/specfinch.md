## Shopify product data monitoring, based on your own rules

Supplier files, PIM and ERP integrations, bulk edits and day-to-day changes can leave a field
empty even when a product page, filter or feed relies on it. The required data differs from
store to store. A generic catalog score cannot define those requirements for your business.

SpecFinch lets you define the fields your catalog needs and checks for missing values. You
decide what to review and make any corrections in Shopify. The app reads product data without
rewriting it.

## Define the required fields for your Shopify catalog

Create up to ten data contracts for all active products, selected product types or collections.
Mark built-in product fields, variant fields and existing metafields as required within each
contract. Your rules describe the data your team expects to be present.

For example, a store might require a material metafield for a product group because its filters
depend on that value. A later import that leaves that field empty can then produce a finding
against that specific rule.

## Keep new missing fields separate from existing cleanup

The first completed full scan establishes a baseline. Existing gaps remain visible as cleanup
work. Later successful scans distinguish newly missing, returned, worsened and resolved findings.
This gives your team a way to review changes without treating the same old backlog as a new
problem every day.

Daily full scans and a manual scan option support regular checks. Filterable findings,
scan history, direct links to Shopify products and CSV exports help you investigate. An optional
email digest reports relevant changes.

## Who is SpecFinch for?

SpecFinch fits Shopify teams that manage a changing catalog through several people or systems
and already know which fields their storefront or downstream processes require. It is useful
when the question is: “What required product data has gone missing since our last successful
check?”

It does not write descriptions, fill metafields or decide which values are correct. Complete
data can support product pages, filters and feeds, but the app does not guarantee search rankings,
conversion or revenue.

## Questions about SpecFinch

### Does SpecFinch change my products or metafields?

No. SpecFinch uses product read access. It identifies missing required data; your team reviews
the findings and makes any corrections in Shopify.

### Can I monitor Shopify metafields?

Yes. Existing product and variant metafields can be marked as required in a data contract,
alongside supported built-in fields. SpecFinch checks whether required data is present.

### What happens to issues that existed before installation?

The first completed full scan records them in the baseline. They remain available as existing
cleanup work, separate from changes detected in later successful scans.

### Can I export findings for my team?

Yes. Findings are filterable and can be exported as CSV. Scan history and direct product links
provide context for follow-up work.

### Is the app available in English?

Yes. SpecFinch's interface is in English. Installation, current pricing and trial details are
available in the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/specfinch).
