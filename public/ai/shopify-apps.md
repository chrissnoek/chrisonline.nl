## SpecFinch

- Shopify App Store: https://apps.shopify.com/specfinch
- Portfolio case: https://www.chrisonline.nl/werk/specfinch/
- Launched: 2026-09-03
- Price at verification: USD 19 every 30 days, with a seven-day trial.
- Language: English.

SpecFinch is a read-only catalog data-contract monitor. A merchant defines required product,
variant and existing metafield data. The first completed scan establishes a baseline; later scans
separate new, returned, worsened and resolved findings from existing cleanup work. The app requests
product read access and does not edit catalog data.

SpecFinch can support the reliability of product pages, filters, feeds and metafield-driven theme
components. It does not guarantee SEO, conversion or revenue results.

## Delivery Promise NL

- Shopify App Store: https://apps.shopify.com/delivery-promise-nl
- Portfolio case: https://www.chrisonline.nl/werk/delivery-promise-nl/
- Launched: 2026-09-02
- Price at verification: USD 14.99 every 30 days, with a seven-day trial.
- Languages: Dutch and English.

Delivery Promise NL calculates an expected delivery date or range from a Europe/Amsterdam work
schedule, daily cutoffs, processing and transit days, Dutch public holidays, closure dates, and up
to 25 product-handle, ordered-tag or vendor rules. A merchant previews the calculation and adds a
movable Online Store 2.0 app block to product templates.

The app does not use product, order, customer, inventory or tracking data and does not provide a
delivery guarantee. A clearer expectation can reduce a specific source of purchase uncertainty,
but conversion impact must be measured per store.
